GymButt Pro

The essentials are free. Pro is for everyone who wants to look deeper into their training.

In short

In Switzerland GymButt Pro costs CHF 3 a month or CHF 25 a year, with a 7-day free trial. It adds reports, strength check, workout generation, a nutrition profile and more. Workouts, avatar, streak and history stay free, without ads.

What does Pro cost?

GymButt Pro is a subscription through Apple, monthly or yearly: CHF 3 a month or CHF 25 a year. The yearly plan is almost a third cheaper than twelve months (CHF 36). The first 7 days are free. Prices in other countries are shown by the App Store in your currency.

The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it at least 24 hours before it ends in your Apple account settings.

What stays free?

The basics are free and ad-free: workouts, over 300 exercises, templates, rest timer, avatar with items, streak, challenges, history with records and muscle map, one Butties group, import from Strong and Hevy and five recipes per diet.

What does Pro add?

  • Generate a workout: pick muscle groups, duration and level, and the workout is ready.
  • Weekly and monthly report: comparison with previous weeks, sets per muscle and a focus for next week.
  • Strength check: a strength level per exercise, goals with a forecast and help on plateaus.
  • Volume trends, recap and CSV export.
  • Recovery per muscle on the muscle map.
  • Nutrition profile: your weight and goal become protein and calories per day and per meal.
  • All recipes instead of five per diet.
  • Two freezes per month instead of one and up to three Butties groups instead of one.

Who is it worth it for?

If you mainly want to log workouts and dress up your Butt, the free version is enough. Pro is worth it if you want to know whether you're getting stronger and where you stall, if you'd rather not plan your own workouts, or if you want your nutrition to follow your goal. Try the 7 days and decide afterwards.

How to cancel

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name at the top, then “Subscriptions” and choose GymButt Pro. You can cancel there at any time. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

With GymButt

The Pro features open where you need them, for example in History under “View stats” for the strength check. You can see what's behind it before you subscribe.

FAQ

How much does GymButt Pro cost?

CHF 3 a month or CHF 25 a year in Switzerland, with a 7-day free trial. In other countries the App Store shows the price in the local currency.

Can I use GymButt without Pro?

Yes. Workouts, exercises, avatar, streak, history and Butties (one group) are free and ad-free.

How do I cancel GymButt Pro?

In your iPhone settings under your name › Subscriptions › GymButt Pro, at least 24 hours before it ends. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.
