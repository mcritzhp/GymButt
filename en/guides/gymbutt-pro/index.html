GymButt Pro

Almost everything is free. Pro gives you CoachButt, your personal coach.

In short

In Switzerland GymButt Pro costs CHF 3 a month or CHF 25 a year, with a 7-day free trial. It adds CoachButt: a personal training and nutrition plan that is adjusted to you every week. Workouts, analytics, the strength check, the nutrition profile and all recipes are free, without ads.

What does Pro cost?

GymButt Pro is a subscription through Apple, monthly or yearly: CHF 3 a month or CHF 25 a year. The yearly plan is almost a third cheaper than twelve months (CHF 36). The first 7 days are free. In other countries the App Store shows prices in your currency.

The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel it in your Apple account settings at least 24 hours before it ends.

What is free?

Almost everything, without ads: workouts, over 300 exercises, templates, rest timer, the avatar with items, streaks with two freezes a month, challenges, history with records and the muscle map, weekly and monthly reports, the strength check, recovery per muscle, volume trends, recap, CSV export, workout generation, the nutrition profile with macros, all recipes, up to three Butties groups and importing from Strong and Hevy.

What does Pro add?

  • CoachButt, your personal plan: you answer a few questions about your goal, experience, training days, time per workout, equipment and diet. That becomes a training plan with exercises, sets and reps and a nutrition plan with matching recipes.
  • Weekly check-in: on Sunday you briefly say how your week went. CoachButt looks at your workouts and adjusts the plan, for example when an exercise stalls or something hurts.
  • Pro items for your Butt: gold crown, Pro shades, reindeer antlers, sparkler, heart sweater and the Gold shine fur.

How does CoachButt work?

The plan is written by an AI, Claude by Anthropic. Before anything is sent for the first time, the app asks for your permission. What is sent are your questionnaire answers and, at the check-in, a summary of your week, without your name or email. The plan then lives on your iPhone. Each month includes two new plans and four check-ins. The privacy policy has the details.

The plan is a suggestion and doesn't replace medical or nutritional advice. If you have pain, injuries or an illness, ask a professional first.

Who is it for?

If you plan your own training, the free version is enough, it now does almost everything. Pro is worth it if you don't want to put a plan together yourself, don't know how to make progress, or want to align training and nutrition with your goal. Try the 7 days and decide afterwards.

How to cancel

In GymButt under Settings › GymButt Pro › "Manage subscription", or in your iPhone settings: tap your name at the top, then "Subscriptions" and GymButt Pro. You can cancel there at any time. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

With GymButt

You'll find CoachButt in the "Coach" tab at the bottom. It also shows you what to expect before you start the subscription.

FAQ

How much does GymButt Pro cost?

CHF 3 a month or CHF 25 a year in Switzerland, with a 7-day free trial. In other countries the App Store shows the price in the local currency.

Can I use GymButt without Pro?

Yes. Workouts, exercises, avatar, streak, history, analytics, the strength check, the nutrition profile, recipes and Butties are free and ad-free. You only need Pro for CoachButt and the Pro items.

What is CoachButt?

Your personal coach in GymButt Pro: your answers become a training and nutrition plan that CoachButt adjusts to your week every Sunday after a short check-in. The plan comes from an AI (Claude by Anthropic) and is not medical advice.

How do I cancel GymButt Pro?

In GymButt under Settings › GymButt Pro › Manage subscription, or in your iPhone settings under your name › Subscriptions, at least 24 hours before it ends. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.
