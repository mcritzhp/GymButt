In short

GymButt is a workout tracker for strength training with over 300 exercises, a rest timer and weight suggestions. On top of that there's Butt: every workout earns XP, a new level and items like caps, hairstyles and fur colors. It keeps you going when motivation runs low.

Three reasons to keep going

Stress-free at the gym

Ready-made workouts for beginners to pros, over 300 exercises with instructions, a rest timer on your Lock Screen and the weight to use next time.

Streaks with rest days

Rest days and freezes protect your streak. Butt only gets lazy after several days without any movement, and one workout brings Butt right back.

Muscle map and records

See which muscles you trained this week, track records per exercise and import your history from Strong or Hevy.

Built for the bench

  • Big buttons, few taps per set
  • Your last weight is already filled in
  • Plate calculator and supersets
  • Cardio, steps from Apple Health
  • Share a workout as an image or send it to Strava

Butties

Start a group with up to five friends and invite them with a code. Your Butts stand side by side, you see who trained this week and cheer each other on. No leaderboard.

Monthly challenge
Complete three tasks in a month to win that month's item.

FAQ

Is GymButt free?

Yes. Workouts, exercises, the avatar and streaks are free and ad-free. The optional GymButt Pro subscription adds weekly and monthly reports, the strength check, workout generation and all recipes.

Do I need an account?

No. Without an account your workout data stays on your iPhone. You only need an account for Butties, the groups with friends.

Is GymButt on Android?

Not yet. GymButt currently runs on iPhone only.
