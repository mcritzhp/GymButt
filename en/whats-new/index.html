What's new in GymButt

App versions and changes, newest first.

In short

The current version is GymButt 1.0.0, the first release on the App Store (November 2026). This page lists what each version adds.

Version 1.0.0 · November 2026

The first release on the App Store, free, with an optional GymButt Pro subscription.

  • Training: over 300 exercises with a figure, instructions and common mistakes, ready-made workouts from beginner to pro, your own templates, weight suggestion, plate calculator, supersets, cardio with time and distance.
  • Rest timer on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Avatar Butt: XP, levels and items like caps, hairstyles, glasses, outfits and fur colors.
  • Streak with rest days and freezes, weekly and monthly challenges.
  • History: calendar, muscle map, records, import from Strong and Hevy (CSV), steps from Apple Health, share a workout as an image or send it to Strava.
  • Butties: private groups of up to 6 people, no leaderboard.
  • Food: over 100 recipes with a daily protein target, shopping list and cooking mode.
  • GymButt Pro: workout generation, reports, strength check, nutrition profile, with a 7-day free trial.
  • In English and German.

Before

Before the App Store release, GymButt was available as a beta through TestFlight. Thanks to everyone who tested.
